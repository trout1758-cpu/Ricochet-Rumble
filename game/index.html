How to play

Rules

  • One hit destroys a tank. Last tank rolling wins the round.
  • Shells ricochet off blocks — and they can hit you too.
  • You only have a few shells in the air at a time.
  • Shells cancel each other out when they collide.
  • Mines blink, then blow up. They go off early if the enemy drives close or someone shoots them. Blasts clear crates.
  • Pits stop tanks, but shells fly straight over.

Phone controller

  • Left thumb: drag to drive.
  • Right thumb: drag to aim, let go to fire. Tap to fire where the turret points. Slide back to the middle to cancel.
  • Mine and Pause buttons are on screen.

Keyboard & gamepad

  • Keys + mouse: WASD drive, mouse aim, click fire, Space / right-click mine.
  • Arrow keys: arrows drive, , . turn turret, / or Enter fire, Right Shift mine.
  • Gamepad: left stick drive, right stick aim, RT/RB/A fire, LT/LB/B mine, Start pause.
