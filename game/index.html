How to play

Modes & power-ups

  • Battle: 2–4 players, Free-for-all or Teams (pick A or B on each card). First to win the set number of rounds. Any player can be the Computer.
  • Co-op campaign: 1–4 players vs 20 stages of CPU tanks, 3 shared lives (+1 every 5 stages). Friendly fire is on!
  • Survival: endless, growing waves in one arena. Knocked-out players respawn while the team has lives.
  • Power-ups: shield (blocks a hit), turbo, triple shot, super bounce, rapid fire. Drive over them.
  • Hats: unlock them with achievements and pick one on your player card.
  • Menus with a controller: the D-pad moves your highlight and A (✕ on PlayStation) presses it. Pressing a drop-down (like the arena) opens a big list to pick from; B cancels. The trigger (RT / R2 / ZR, or B on a Wii Remote) is always Ready. The first player can reach the whole screen; everyone else sets up their own card. Start starts the match.
  • More players: press a button on a free gamepad or Wii Remote to join, or use + Add player / + Add Computer.

Rules

  • One hit destroys a tank. Last tank rolling wins the round.
  • Shells ricochet off blocks — and they can hit you too.
  • You only have a few shells in the air at a time.
  • Shells cancel each other out when they collide.
  • Mines blink, then blow up. They go off early if the enemy drives close or someone shoots them. Blasts clear crates.
  • Pits stop tanks, but shells fly straight over.
  • Most themes add a twist: ice, black holes, eruptions, tides, bouncy blocks, sandstorms or regrowing planters (switch them off in the lobby).

Phone controller

  • Any phone (iPhone or Android): scan the lobby's Scan to join QR code with the phone's camera. Phones join in the order they scan, on the next open spot (up to four). The phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as this computer.
  • Left thumb: drag to drive.
  • Right thumb: drag to aim, let go to fire. Tap to fire where the turret points. Slide back to the middle to cancel.
  • Mine is the button in the top middle; Pause is top right.
  • In the lobby, a Wi-Fi phone shows your own player card: tap your colour, hat and team, type your name, then tap Ready.

Wii Remote + Nunchuk

  • Plug in the Mayflash DolphinBar, set it to mode 4 (press its mode button until LED 4 lights), and sync each Wii Remote to it (press the bar's sync button, then the red button on the remote). Put the bar above or below the screen; it is the sensor bar too.
  • Pick Wii Remote + Nunchuk (DolphinBar) in a player card's controller menu. The first time, the browser asks you to allow “Nintendo RVL-CNT-01”. If several remotes are free, press A on the one you want.
  • Nunchuk stick: drive. Point at the screen: aim (needs a sensor bar — without one, tilt the remote to aim).
  • B or Z fire, A or C mine, + pause. In menus, point and press A to click.

Keyboard & gamepad

  • Keys + mouse: WASD drive, mouse aim, click fire, Space / right-click mine.
  • Arrow keys: arrows drive, , . turn turret, / or Enter fire, Right Shift mine.
  • Controllers: pick Controller on a player card, then press a button on the one you want (Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro, Joy-Cons, other gamepads, or a Wii Remote). Or just press a button on a free controller to join. Xbox / PlayStation / Switch controllers: left stick (or D-pad) drive, right stick aim. Fire: RT/RB/A (Xbox), R2/R1/✕ (PlayStation), ZR/R/B (Switch). Mine: LT/LB/B, L2/L1/○, ZL/L/A. Start/Options/+ pauses.
  • Other gamepads and single Joy-Cons: stick or D-pad drives; with no second stick the turret points the way you drive.
